Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Ritratto (“we”) collects when you use Ritratto, why we collect it, and your rights under Quebec's Law 25 (Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector).

1. Information we collect

  • Photos you upload — used only to generate your memorial portrait.
  • Contact details — your email address and business name when you create a funeral-home account.
  • Payment information — processed securely by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • Newsletter consent — whether you chose to receive product updates by email.
  • Anonymous visit counts — we count how many times our pages are viewed each day. These counts contain no personal information: no IP addresses, no cookies, no identifiers.

2. How we use your photos

  • Your photo is sent to our AI image provider (Google's Gemini API) for the sole purpose of creating your portrait.
  • We never sell your photos, publish them, or use them to train AI models.
  • Every uploaded photo and generated portrait is permanently deleted from our servers 24 hours after it is created.

3. Newsletter and update emails

  • We send product news and updates only if you tick the opt-in box when signing up. Consent is never assumed.
  • Each email contains an unsubscribe link. You can withdraw your consent at any time, free of charge.
  • We never sell or rent your email address.

4. Your rights (Quebec Law 25)

  • You may request access to your personal information, or ask us to correct or delete it, at any time by writing to antonio.learningisfun@gmail.com.
  • Person responsible for the protection of personal information: Antonio Daniele, antonio.learningisfun@gmail.com.

5. Data retention

Photos and portraits: 24 hours, then permanently deleted. Photos uploaded to customize a lamp — or an AI portrait you choose to put on a lamp — are kept as part of your order so we can make your lamp. Account information: kept until you ask us to delete it. Payment records are retained by Stripe according to its own policies.

6. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above.

Questions? Contact us at antonio.learningisfun@gmail.com.
