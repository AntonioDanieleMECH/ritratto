About

One engineer, one workshop.

I'm Antonio Daniele, a mechanical engineering student at McGill and the founder of ADN Creations. I built my first 3D printer at ten and I have been designing and making things for nine years.

Everything is designed in house in LaSalle, Montreal, and printed from plant-based materials. Additive manufacturing keeps waste to a minimum, and every piece starts from a blank page in my own design files.

My devotional pieces are carried in the boutique of Saint Joseph's Oratory in Montreal.

The goal is to grow ADN Creations into an engineering design company: new products, new departments, and manufacturing that stays local.
