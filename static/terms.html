Refund & Purchase Terms

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Ritratto is a service run by ADN Creations, Montreal, Quebec, Canada.

1. One-time payments — no subscriptions

Every purchase on Ritratto is a single one-time payment. There are no subscriptions, no accounts to manage, and no recurring charges.

2. Pay only after you love your preview

Your watermarked preview is always free and takes about 30 seconds. You only pay when you decide to download the full-resolution portrait — never before you've seen it.

3. What your purchase includes

  • Memorial portrait ($9.99): one high-resolution PNG portrait of your loved one in formal attire, with no watermark.
  • Photo refinement ($4.99): one restored high-resolution PNG of your photo — scratches, fading, and blur repaired; the person and everything else stay exactly the same.
  • 4-pack ($29.97): four memorial portraits — four for the price of three. Each portrait includes up to 5 free preview versions and one HD download.
  • Checkout is processed securely by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.

4. Your rights to the portrait

Your purchase includes personal printing and sharing rights: print the portrait for the service, frame it for your home, and share it with family. The portrait is made from your photo, which belongs to you.

5. Refunds

  • If a technical problem prevents your download, or your file arrives corrupted, email us and we will fix it or refund you — your choice.
  • Because you pay only after approving your free preview, we cannot offer refunds for a change of mind after a successful download.
  • Memorial lamps are made to order just for you. If your lamp arrives damaged, or doesn't match the design you approved, email us a photo and we will make it right.

6. Download window — save your file right away

Your portrait and uploaded photos are permanently deleted from our servers 24 hours after creation, as described in our privacy policy. Download your file immediately after purchase and keep your own backup — we cannot recover it after deletion.
